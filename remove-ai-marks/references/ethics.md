# Privacy and Hygiene on Content Ownership

blotless is designed to **protect privacy and hygiene** on content that you own or have permission to process: strip forensic Unicode, stamps, and hard-bound C2PA/metadata; optionally paraphrase statistical marks.

Do **not**:

- Claim the result is certified "human-written" or that a vendor detector will fail
- Use the tool to engage in academic fraud, evade lawful disclosure, or misrepresent provenance in regulated filings
- Process other people’s files without authorization

Layer A removals are verifiable, while Layer B is a best-effort approach that may degrade voice quality. If the quality of the content is more important than hygiene, skip Layer B.

Responsible use: adhere to local laws. Authors release the tool under no warranty regarding misuse.
